New to karting? You're in the right place.

You don't need a racing background to enjoy a race weekend. Here's the short version.

01

What's LO206?

A popular four-stroke kart racing class built around closely matched engines. It's a great place to see driving skill, racecraft, and setup make a difference.

02

What happens at a race?

Practice, qualifying, and racing — plus a lot of wrenching, chatting, and figuring things out in the pits.

03

Can I come watch?

Absolutely. Check the event organizer's spectator details, stop by the paddock when access is permitted, and say hello.
